# *The Lovecraft Investigations*-Style Recommendations

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Basis:** Strong positive response to *The Lovecraft Investigations*.

## What makes the target style work

The best matches are not simply “horror podcasts”. They should ideally combine:

- A credible documentary, journalism or recovered-recordings frame
- An investigation that expands from a local mystery into a wider system
- Occult, folkloric, conspiratorial, scientific or cosmic material
- Deliberate pacing and a coherent serial arc
- Strong acting, writing, sound design and atmosphere
- A sense that the world is larger, older and less rational than the investigators understand

Some recommendations below are exact format matches; others preserve the investigative structure, institutional paranoia or cosmic dread while using a more conventional audio-drama form.

## Start with the Pleasant Green universe

These are the closest possible follow-ups because they come from Julian Simpson, the writer/director of *The Lovecraft Investigations*, and share its strange British espionage/occult world.

### Mythos

Three tightly made episodes involving British intelligence, folklore, magic, time travel and the mysterious Department. It is more overtly spy-fiction and playful than *The Lovecraft Investigations*, but it has the same confidence, cast, sound design and escalating weirdness. The 2025 version has been remixed with new music. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/mythos/id1850157074) or explore the [full-series page](https://www.cartoongravity.com/mythos-the-full-series/).

### Who Is Aldrich Kemp?

A five-part BBC adventure about an art thief, a missing child and an occult mystery moving from London to Paris, New York, Amsterdam and beyond. It is lighter and more flamboyant than *The Lovecraft Investigations*, but uses many of the same voices and the same pleasure in hidden organisations, impossible histories and British spy-fi. [Julian Simpson's launch page](https://www.cartoongravity.com/20220218-morning-pages/) · [BBC Limelight](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0bpwb7w).

### The Pleasant Green prologue arc

For a deeper dive, listen to *Fragments*, *The Listener*, *Bad Memories*, *Kokomo*, *Fugue State* and then *Mythos*. These are shorter, looser dramas that gradually establish the ideas and institutions later used by *The Lovecraft Investigations*. The [full Mythos page](https://www.cartoongravity.com/mythos-the-full-series/) and Julian Simpson's [audio archive](https://www.cartoongravity.com/julian-simpson/) are the best starting points. Availability and ordering can vary between feeds.

## Closest to the podcast-within-a-podcast form

### The Battersea Poltergeist

A BBC investigation into a real 1950s London haunting, combining present-day interviews and analysis with dramatised reconstructions. It is the closest recommendation to *The Lovecraft Investigations*'s balance between apparently serious investigation and increasingly unstable supernatural possibilities. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-battersea-poltergeist/id1548946506).

### Limetown

An investigative journalist for the fictional American Public Radio investigates the disappearance of more than 300 people from a Tennessee research town. It is less occult than *The Lovecraft Investigations*, but its journalistic framing, institutional secrecy and gradually widening conspiracy are an almost direct formal match. [Listen via Two-Up Productions](https://twoupproductions.com/limetown/podcast) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/limetown/id1024247322).

### The Black Tapes

A journalist investigates a paranormal researcher who has never failed to debunk a case—except for a small group of unsolved “black tapes”. This is one of the clearest precursors to the *Lovecraft Investigations* format: a reporter, a sceptical expert, strange cases and a larger occult pattern. The caveat is that the plotting becomes uneven and the ending is divisive. [Official site](https://theblacktapespodcast.com/new-page-1) · [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-black-tapes/id997522893).

### Uncanny

Danny Robins investigates real accounts of ghosts, UFOs and other paranormal experiences, testing them through interviews, expert analysis and reconstructed scenes. It is factual rather than fictional, but the mixture of investigation, ambiguity and dramatic sound design makes it a good companion listen. [Listen via BBC Podcasts](https://lnk.to/uncanny-pod).

## Found recordings, archives and cursed media

### Archive 81

A found-footage horror podcast about ritual, cities, sound and the subconscious. The archive format and the way apparently mundane recordings open into an occult cosmology make this one of the strongest matches for the structural-horror side of *The Lovecraft Investigations*. [Official site](https://www.archive81.com/) · [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/archive-81/id1098194172).

### The Sheridan Tapes

A disgraced detective reconstructs the disappearance of a horror writer from a box of cassette tapes documenting her paranormal investigations. It is more conventionally acted than *The Lovecraft Investigations*, but the missing-person frame, scattered recordings and widening existential threat are very close. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-sheridan-tapes/id1508614133).

### The Polybius Conspiracy

A seven-part audio docudrama about the alleged 1980s arcade game Polybius and the two men whose lives become connected to the legend. It blends urban myth, missing people, obsession and recovered-media texture in a compact, finished series. [Listen via Radiotopia](https://www.radiotopia.fm/podcasts/the-polybius-conspiracy) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-polybius-conspiracy/id1640573819).

### The Deca Tapes

Leaked recordings reveal ten people confined in the same unknown space, with each tape offering a different fragment of the truth. This is more science-fictional and puzzle-box-like, but the constrained evidence, unreliable disclosures and careful sound design should appeal to the archival side of *The Lovecraft Investigations*. The first season is a self-contained story. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-deca-tapes/id1455127076).

## Cosmic, folkloric and institutional horror

### The Silt Verses

Two worshippers of an outlawed river-god travel through a world where religion, law and state power are inseparable. It is full-cast weird fiction rather than faux documentary, but it is exceptionally strong on religious bureaucracy, rural isolation, body horror and systems that consume human beings. [Listen via the official site](https://www.thesiltverses.com/listen-now) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-silt-verses/id1547222295).

### I Am In Eskew

A man trapped in an impossible, constantly changing city makes recordings in the hope that somebody outside will hear them. It is more abstract and oppressive than *The Lovecraft Investigations*, with a single narrator and heavy body horror, but it captures the sense of a place governed by an unknowable intelligence. [Episode guide and warnings](https://www.iamineskew.com/episodes) · [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/i-am-in-eskew/id1339770338).

### The White Vault

Recovered records from a repair team sent to a remote Arctic outpost gradually reveal something beneath the ice. It has multilingual field recordings, scientific detail, isolation, excellent sound design and a slow expansion from practical survival into non-human horror. [Official site](https://www.foolandscholar.com/the-white-vault-1) · [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-white-vault/id1267043823).

### Tracks

A BBC conspiracy thriller in which a woman’s mysterious pregnancy leads into a scientific and political mystery. It is not explicitly Lovecraftian, but its investigative structure, clinical unease and award-winning sound design make it a strong fit for the “credible investigation into something impossible” aspect. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/tracks/id1306904582).

## Lower-priority but potentially rewarding

### TANIS

A journalist investigates an ancient mystery in the Pacific Northwest, moving through cults, secret research, esoteric history and conspiracy. It has a very close documentary-podcast surface, but it is sprawling and much less disciplined than *The Lovecraft Investigations*. Try it only if the atmosphere and mythology matter more than closure. [Official site](https://tanispodcast.com/) · [About the show](https://tanispodcast.com/about/).

### Tales from the Black Vault

An anthology of Lovecraft-inspired audio dramas with dialogue, music and sound effects. It is less serialised and less journalistic, but useful when the specific requirement is literary cosmic horror in audio form. [Official site](https://www.blackvault.net/).

## Suggested order

1. *Mythos*
2. *Who Is Aldrich Kemp?*
3. *The Battersea Poltergeist*
4. *Limetown*
5. *The Polybius Conspiracy*
6. *Archive 81*
7. *The Silt Verses*
8. *I Am In Eskew*
9. *The Sheridan Tapes*
10. *Tracks*

Content warnings apply particularly to *The Silt Verses*, *I Am In Eskew*, *The White Vault* and *The Sheridan Tapes*, which include body horror, violence, death, religious trauma and existential dread.
