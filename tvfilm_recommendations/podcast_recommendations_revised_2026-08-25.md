# Revised Podcast Recommendations

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Starting point:** Enjoyed *West Cork*; recommendations also informed by the dated TV/film notes and the longer-lived preference index.

## What the list is optimising for

The subject does not need to be Irish or criminal. The strongest matches combine several of these qualities:

- Finite, substantial series with deliberate pacing
- Investigative or documentary structure
- Strong atmosphere and sense of place
- Systems under pressure: state secrecy, institutional failure, corruption, bureaucracy or corporate power
- Character-driven reporting and moral ambiguity
- Serious, occasionally bleak tone
- Distinctive audio form, including archives, mockumentary, field recordings or found documents

## Start here

### The Great Post Office Trial

The clearest match for the interest in institutional rot: a detailed account of the British Post Office scandal, following the evidence, the technology, the legal fight and the people damaged by an organisation insisting that its system could not be wrong. Start with the [official site](https://www.postofficetrial.com/) or the [Apple Podcasts feed](https://podcasts.apple.com/ie/podcast/the-great-post-office-trial/id1569800816).

### S-Town

The best match for *West Cork*'s sense of place and complicated human portrait. It begins as a possible murder investigation and becomes a novelistic study of one man, one Alabama town and the mysteries embedded in a life. Listen at the [official site](https://stownpodcast.org/about).

### Atlantic

The closest Irish structural match: a three-part Irish Times investigation into the unidentified man who died in Sligo in 2009. [Listen via the Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/podcasts/atlantic/).

### Tunnel 29

A ten-part historical thriller about an escape tunnel under the Berlin Wall, built from survivor interviews, Stasi documents and recordings from the tunnel. It has the procedural momentum and physical atmosphere of a very good historical drama. Start with the [creator's podcast page](https://helenamerriman.com/radio-and-podcasts/) or [BBC/Apple episode listing](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/tunnel-29-8-the-messenger/id1741328387?i=1000652496006).

### The Lovecraft Investigations

The strongest audio-fiction fit: a high-production BBC mockumentary in which a true-crime podcast investigation opens onto occult conspiracy and cosmic horror. Listen on [BBC Sounds](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p06spb8w) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-lovecraft-investigations/id1444464816).

## Institutions, state power and political systems

### GUBU

Seven-part Irish Times account of the Malcolm Macarthur murders and the political crisis that nearly brought down Charles Haughey's government. The best earlier recommendation for crime, scandal and political atmosphere. [Listen via the Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/podcasts/gubu/).

### The Forgotten: Dublin Monaghan Bombings 1974

RTÉ's six-part investigation into the bombings, the failed investigations and the unanswered questions surrounding official responsibility. [Listen via RTÉ Radio 1](https://www.rte.ie/radio/radio1/the-forgotten/).

### Bed of Lies

The strongest British state-conspiracy recommendation. The current feed includes the Troubles-focused *Conflict* series, alongside earlier investigations into undercover policing and the infected-blood scandal. It is investigative journalism built around deception, collusion and institutional self-protection. [Listen via The Telegraph's feed](https://shows.acast.com/bed-of-lies) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/bed-of-lies/id1537575625).

### The Trojan Horse Affair

An eight-part investigation into the anonymous letter that triggered a political panic about Islamic extremism in Birmingham schools. The real subject becomes the interaction of local grievance, official inquiries, media narratives and national policy. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-trojan-horse-affair/id1606918193).

### In the Dark — Season 2

An exceptionally meticulous investigation into why Curtis Flowers was tried six times for the same crime. It moves beyond the individual case into prosecutorial power, racial injustice and a criminal-justice system that can reproduce its own assumptions. Start with the [APM Reports series page](https://features.apmreports.org/in-the-dark/season-two/) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/in-the-dark/id1148175292).

### The Coming Storm

BBC journalist Gabriel Gatehouse investigates QAnon, conspiracy culture and the political world surrounding the January 6 insurrection. This is the best match for deep-state paranoia as a real social phenomenon rather than a fictional plot. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-coming-storm/id1601195264).

### The Lazarus Heist

An investigation into the North Korean hacking operation accused of stealing money from banks, film studios and other targets around the world. Recommended for the combination of cybercrime, geopolitics, state secrecy and procedural reporting. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-lazarus-heist/id1561990291).

### The Missing Cryptoqueen

The story of OneCoin, a global cryptocurrency fraud and the disappearance of its central figure. It has the scale of a conspiracy thriller but is grounded in reporting, financial mechanisms and the people pulled into the scheme. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-missing-cryptoqueen/id1480370173).

### Nice White Parents

A five-part investigation into how ostensibly liberal parents exercise power inside a New York public school. A compact study of class, race, institutional language and the gap between stated intentions and structural effects. [Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/nice-white-parents/id1524080195).

## Place, personality and moral ambiguity

### The Nobody Zone

An RTÉ/Third Ear six-part series about an Irish serial killer operating among London's homeless population. Its strongest subject is not the killer but the social world in which people can disappear without attracting attention. [Read about and listen to it via RTÉ](https://about.rte.ie/2020/02/18/the-nobody-zone-new-original-podcast-series-for-rte/).

### The Witness: In His Own Words

An Irish documentary built around the first-person account of a protected witness whose testimony helped convict gangland murderers. Recommended for unusual access, an unstable moral perspective and a character-led structure. [Listen at the official site](https://thewitnesspodcast.com/).

### The Outlaw Ocean

An international investigative anthology about crime, exploitation and lawlessness at sea. The geography changes from episode to episode, but the underlying subject is the space created when law, labour regulation and accountability stop functioning. [Listen at the official site](https://theoutlawocean.com/podcast).

## Audio fiction and documentary-form horror

### The Magnus Archives

Horror fiction presented as an archive of statements, gradually revealing a system of existential fears attempting to breach reality. Recommended for cumulative world-building and bureaucratic documentation used as narrative form. [Listen via Rusty Quill](https://rustyquill.com/show/the-magnus-archives/).

### Video Palace

Investigative audio fiction about rare occult VHS tapes, collectors and the shadow network surrounding them. Recommended for analogue atmosphere, conspiratorial escalation and cursed-media logic. Listen on [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/video-palace/id1439247558); background is available from [AMC/Shudder](https://www.amcglobalmedia.com/2018/10/22/shudder-debuts-first-original-podcast-series-with-the-chilling-thrill-ride-video-palace-from-the-makers-of-the-blair-witch-project/).

### The White Vault

Found-footage horror about a repair team at a remote Arctic outpost, told through collected records, multiple languages and detailed sound design. The isolation, research setting and buried non-human threat make it the best new recommendation for the *The Terror* / cosmic-dread side of the profile. [Listen via Fool & Scholar](https://www.foolandscholar.com/the-white-vault-1) or [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-white-vault/id1267043823).
