# Research: Small-Form-Factor Android Tablets at CeX Dublin
**Date:** Saturday 28 June 2026
**Focus:** Compact tablets (7–9") suitable for reading while travelling; fits in a jacket pocket

## Context: The Nexus 7 Benchmark

The Google Nexus 7 (2013) is the reference point for this use case — a tablet that genuinely fits in a jacket pocket and is comfortable to hold one-handed for extended reading.

| Device | Height | Width | Thickness | Weight | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nexus 7 (2013)** | 200mm | **114mm** | 8.65mm | 290g | 7.0" |
| Nexus 7 (2012) | 198mm | 120mm | 10.5mm | 340g | 7.0" |
| Samsung Tab A9 (8.7") | 210mm | ~124–129mm | **6.9mm** | 341g | 8.7" |
| Samsung Tab A7 Lite (8.7") | 212mm | 124.7mm | 8.0mm | 366g | 8.7" |
| Amazon Fire HD 8 (2022) | 202mm | 137mm | 9.7mm | 337g | 8.0" |

**Width is the critical dimension for jacket pocket fit.** The Nexus 7 (2013) was unusually narrow at 114mm. Modern options are 10–25mm wider — noticeable in a pocket but manageable depending on the jacket.

## ℹ️ CeX Grading

- **Grade A:** Mint condition, original box and accessories included. Highest price.
- **Grade B:** Good condition. Light cosmetic marks on casing, not visible with the screen on. Compatible charger included (no box). **Best value.**
- **Grade C:** Working but significant cosmetic wear. Compatible charger included. Cheapest option.
